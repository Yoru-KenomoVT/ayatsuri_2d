# Code of conduct

Ayatsuri2D is made by artists, riggers and programmers for artists, riggers
and programmers. Plenty of people here are new to rigging, new to open
source, or writing in their second or third language. Be the person you
would want to meet on your first day.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people whose
  English is not their first language.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Critique a rig, a patch or an idea;
  never someone's worth.
- Assume good faith. If something reads as rude, ask what was meant before
  you answer it.
- Credit people. Name who drew the art, who found the bug, who wrote the
  fix.
- Accept that a "no" on a feature or a pull request is about the project's
  direction, not about you.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or jokes at someone's expense, in public or
  in private messages.
- Unwanted sexual attention or content, including sexualised art posted
  where nobody asked for it.
- Sharing someone's private information, or anything they sent you
  privately, without their permission.
- Posting models, art or files you do not have the right to share. A
  VTuber's model is their face and their livelihood; leaked or ripped
  models are not welcome here in any form.
- Pushing anyone to move past a "no", or keeping on after being asked to
  stop.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: GitHub issues, pull requests and discussions,
the Discord server, and anywhere you speak as part of the project.

## Reporting

If someone makes you or anyone else feel unsafe or unwelcome, tell a
maintainer privately:

- on Discord ([discord.gg/WyzDsXzdnh](https://discord.gg/WyzDsXzdnh)),
  in a direct message to a team member;
- on GitHub, with **Report content** on the comment or issue, or a
  private message to a maintainer.

Reports are kept confidential. You will get an answer, and you will not be
punished for reporting in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what to do, in proportion to what happened: a
private word, a public correction, a temporary ban, or a permanent one. A
maintainer who is part of the report steps out of the decision.
